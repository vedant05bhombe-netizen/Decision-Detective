COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT
AstraNova Technologies Pvt Ltd

1. INTRODUCTION

This Company Policy document defines the core operational, human resource, security, and governance guidelines followed by AstraNova Technologies Pvt Ltd. The objective of this policy is to ensure a transparent, ethical, and secure working environment while aligning employee conduct with organizational goals and legal requirements.

All employees, contractors, and authorized vendors are required to comply with the policies outlined in this document. Failure to adhere to these policies may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment or contracts.

---

2. EMPLOYMENT AND HR POLICY

AstraNova Technologies Pvt Ltd follows fair employment practices and provides equal opportunities to all employees regardless of gender, religion, caste, or background. Recruitment and promotions are strictly based on merit, experience, performance ratings, and organizational requirements.

Employees are categorized as permanent, probationary, or contractual based on their employment agreement. Probationary employees are subject to limited benefits until confirmation.

Promotion decisions are governed by defined promotion rules, including minimum experience, performance rating thresholds, and availability of budget. Interns and temporary staff are not eligible for promotions.

---

3. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

The company operates on a hybrid working model. Standard business hours are from 09:30 AM to 06:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to adhere to assigned working schedules as defined by their department.

Attendance tracking is mandatory, and repeated instances of absenteeism or unapproved leave may result in corrective action.

---

4. LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to annual leave, sick leave, and emergency leave as defined in the official leave policy. Leave requests must be submitted through the company’s HR system and are subject to managerial approval.

Leave during critical business periods such as financial year-end months may be restricted. Carry forward of unused leave is allowed within defined limits.

Misuse of sick leave or excessive unplanned leave may be reviewed by HR and management.

---

5. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

Employee performance is evaluated periodically through structured performance reviews conducted by assigned managers. Ratings are based on job responsibilities, teamwork, communication, and contribution to organizational objectives.

Performance reviews directly influence promotions, salary revisions, and training recommendations. Consistently poor performance may lead to performance improvement plans or further disciplinary measures.

---

6. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

Compensation packages are structured according to industry standards and internal pay scales. Salaries are confidential and must not be disclosed among employees.

Salary revisions and bonuses are subject to performance outcomes, company profitability, and budget approvals.

---

7. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times. Harassment, discrimination, abuse of authority, or unethical conduct will not be tolerated.

Use of company assets must be strictly for business purposes. Any misuse of company resources, including data or systems, is considered a serious violation.

---

8. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Information security is a critical priority. Employees must protect company data, credentials, and systems from unauthorized access.

Strong passwords, two-factor authentication, VPN access for remote work, and encrypted data storage are mandatory as per security rules. Access to sensitive systems is restricted based on department and role.

Any suspected security incident must be reported immediately to the IT Security team.

---

9. DATA PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

All employee, customer, and business data must be handled with confidentiality. Sharing confidential information with unauthorized parties is strictly prohibited.

Employees must comply with applicable data protection laws and internal data handling guidelines.

---

10. VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY POLICY

All vendors and third parties must comply with company procurement, security, and legal policies. Vendor selection is based on performance rating, cost, compliance history, and security audits where applicable.

Blacklisted vendors are strictly prohibited from engagement.

---

11. BUDGET AND FINANCIAL CONTROLS

Each department operates within an allocated budget approved by management. Overspending beyond approved limits requires formal approval.

Quarterly budget reviews are conducted to ensure financial discipline and sustainability.

---

12. DISCIPLINARY ACTION

Violation of company policies may result in disciplinary actions ranging from warnings to termination, depending on the severity of the offense.

All disciplinary actions are conducted fairly, transparently, and in accordance with internal procedures.

---

13. POLICY COMPLIANCE AND AMENDMENTS

Employees are responsible for understanding and complying with all company policies. The company reserves the right to amend policies as required due to legal, operational, or strategic changes.

Updated policies will be communicated through official channels.

---

14. CONCLUSION

This Company Policy document serves as a guiding framework for ethical conduct, operational excellence, and professional growth. Compliance with these policies ensures a safe, secure, and productive work environment for all stakeholders.

All employees are required to acknowledge and adhere to this policy as a condition of employment.

END OF DOCUMENT
